# Sound credits

Every pack is cut from a recording shared on [Freesound](https://freesound.org/). `tools/cut_recordings.py` holds the exact cuts; the recordings themselves aren't in this repository.

| Pack | Recording | Author | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Busy Dev | [Computer keyboard typing and keystrokes - Apple MacBook Pro 2018](https://freesound.org/people/khenshom/sounds/565645/) | khenshom | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| Newsroom | [Typewriter, Olivetti Lettra 22](https://freesound.org/people/keithpeter/sounds/123344/) | keithpeter | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| In the Zone | [Typing on a keyboard](https://freesound.org/people/SamsterBirdies/sounds/489424/) | SamsterBirdies | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| Lounge | [Piano loops 213 octave down long loop 120 bpm](https://freesound.org/people/josefpres/sounds/871796/) | josefpres | [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) |
| Vienna | [260130_203323-3_FR_Amateur_orchestra_playing_Mozart](https://freesound.org/people/kevp888/sounds/848762/) | kevp888 | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |

**Changes:** each recording was cut into short clips, mixed down to mono, faded at both ends and set to a common loudness. The keyboard clips are also softly limited, the Busy Dev clips are noise-gated, and the Newsroom bell is filtered to its own pitches. The Vienna clips are adapted from kevp888's recording under CC BY 4.0.

`tick.wav` is synthesized by `tools/make_sounds.py`.
